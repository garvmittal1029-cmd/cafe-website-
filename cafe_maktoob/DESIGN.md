## Brand & Style

The design system for this product is rooted in **Artisanal Minimalism**, specifically tailored for a high-end café experience. The brand personality is "Graceful, Intentional, and Grounded," evoking the sensory richness of slow-brewed coffee and the tactile elegance of a boutique stationery shop. 

The aesthetic leverages **Tonal Layering** and **Editorial Precision**. It moves away from generic digital patterns toward a layout that feels like a premium, printed coffee journal. The visual narrative is built on the concept of "The Written Word & The Perfect Pour," utilizing generous whitespace, sophisticated serif typography, and a "Cream and Roast" color philosophy to create an emotional response of calm luxury and exceptional craft.

## Colors

The palette is a monochromatic study in browns, designed to feel "steeped" in quality.

*   **Primary (Deep Espresso):** Used for maximum contrast in typography and primary navigation. It provides the "ink" on the page.
*   **Secondary (Mahogany):** Used for structural elements and secondary actions, providing warmth and depth.
*   **Tertiary (Warm Cocoa):** Used for accents, dividers, and decorative icons.
*   **Neutral (Milk-Froth White):** A high-end, off-white surface color that prevents the clinical feel of pure white, serving as the "parchment" for the brand.
*   **Accent (Latte Gold):** Reserved exclusively for high-value highlights, such as award badges or premium selection indicators.

All prices must be displayed in Rupees (₹) using the Primary color for prominence.

## Typography

The typography system is an elegant pairing of a high-contrast serif and a modern, sharp grotesque.

*   **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Refined with increased tracking for an airy, graceful feel. Large display sizes should use a tighter line height to create a "block" editorial effect.
*   **Body & UI (Hanken Grotesk):** Chosen for its contemporary precision. It offers a cleaner, more sophisticated counterpoint than standard geometric sans-serifs, ensuring readability for ingredient lists and descriptions.
*   **Labels:** Always set in Hanken Grotesk. Use uppercase for category headers and navigation items with 10% letter-spacing to reinforce the artisanal packaging aesthetic.
*   **Price Rendering:** Prices (₹) should be rendered in Hanken Grotesk with medium weight to ensure the currency symbol remains crisp and legible.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system utilizes a **Fixed Editorial Grid** to maintain a sense of curated composition.

*   **Desktop:** A 12-column grid with wide 32px gutters. Content is centered within a 1280px container, with generous "safe-zone" margins (64px) to create a premium, uncrowded feel.
*   **Mobile:** A 4-column grid with 24px margins.
*   **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a strict 8px base. Section gaps should be aggressive (120px+) to allow the eye to rest between different menu categories or brand stories.
*   **Asymmetry:** Use intentional asymmetrical padding for image-and-text blocks to mimic the layout of high-end lifestyle magazines.

## Elevation & Depth

Hierarchy is established through **Tonal Tiers** and **Soft Diffusion** rather than physical shadows.

*   **Tonal Layering:** The primary background is "Milk-Froth White" (#FDFCFB). Containers and cards use subtle shifts in tone—either a slightly "dimmed" cream or a very light "Warm Cocoa" wash—to indicate layering.
*   **Shadows:** When necessary for interaction, use "Mocha Shadows." These are extremely diffused (24px+ blur), low-opacity (5%), and tinted with the Primary color (`rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.05)`).
*   **Ghost Borders:** Use 0.5px or 1px strokes in a "Mahogany" shade at 10-15% opacity to define containers without breaking the organic flow of the page.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Soft & Sophisticated**. 

The design avoids the "bubbliness" of high-roundedness and the "harshness" of sharp corners. A subtle 0.25rem (4px) base roundedness is used for most UI elements to provide a "hand-finished" quality, similar to the edge of a custom leather menu or a stone coaster. Images should use a slightly more pronounced radius (0.75rem) to soften the visual weight of photography.

## Components

*   **Buttons:** Primary buttons are "Deep Espresso" with "Froth-White" text. They use minimal rounding (4px) and Hanken Grotesk Bold. Secondary buttons are "Ghost" style—1px Mahogany borders with no fill.
*   **Cards:** Menu cards are borderless, using a subtle "Warm Cocoa" background tint. The product name is in Playfair Display, with the price (₹) right-aligned in Hanken Grotesk.
*   **Input Fields:** Minimalist design—only a bottom-border (1px Mahogany) with Hanken Grotesk for input text. Floating labels should be used to maintain a clean "ledger" look.
*   **Chips & Tags:** Small, rectangular tags with 2px rounding. Use Mahogany for the background with 10% opacity and Primary brown for the text.
*   **Price Displays:** All pricing must be prefixed with the ₹ symbol. Avoid decimal points for a cleaner, high-end look (e.g., ₹450 instead of ₹450.00) unless precision is required.
*   **Specialty Component - "The Signature Line":** A thin horizontal rule ending in a small decorative diamond or coffee-bean icon, used to separate major sections or end a long-form story.
